# Analytics findings — Search Console export of 2026-08-17

Source: three Search Console "Performance on Search" exports the site
owner uploaded to Drive — 2026-07-14, 2026-08-07 and 2026-08-17. The
last one covers the **last 3 months** (2026-06-20 → 2026-08-17) and is
the basis for everything below. The earlier two were merged to build the
daily timeline.

The raw CSVs are in `analytics/inbox/`.

## The headline

**Half of every impression the site earns is a cloud-cost query. None
of them click.**

| Query theme | Impressions | Share | Clicks | Avg position |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cloud cost / spend | 232 | **50.1%** | **0** | 75.8 |
| sysadmin / linux | 52 | 11.2% | 0 | 71.7 |
| password managers | 49 | 10.6% | 0 | 55.3 |
| phones / iphone | 47 | 10.2% | 0 | 33.4 |
| network security | 26 | 5.6% | 0 | 69.4 |
| other | 26 | 5.6% | 0 | 60.9 |
| senior tech (general) | 21 | 4.5% | 0 | 84.2 |
| ai troubleshooting | 10 | 2.2% | 0 | 70.7 |

Position 75.8 is page eight of Google. The site is visible for exactly
one topic and the searcher is seven pages away.

The single page serving that cluster —
`/sysadmin/how-reduce-cloud-spend-before-year-end-2025/` — has **251
impressions, 0 clicks, average position 74.74**. It is the biggest page
on the site by impressions and it earns nothing.

The reason is in the title: *"How to Actually Reduce Your Cloud Spend
Before Year-End 2025"*. The post was published in December 2025 and
framed around a deadline that passed eleven months ago. The queries it
is being shown for still say "2025" because that is what the title
matches, and that is the whole problem.

**The fix landed after this data.** "Cloud for Cheap: What It Actually
Costs to Run Your Own" went live on 2026-11-10 and is the second entry
in this cluster. The cluster now has an anchor and a supporting post
instead of one expired one.

Two of these are title problems rather than ranking problems. The
iPhone setup post has a good title but its first line promises a
"30-Minute Setup" — a lot to read for a quick click. The
is-chatgpt-reading-your-parents-data post ranks at **16.19**, which is
page two, and loses all 79 impressions there.

The homepage at position 6.08 with 38 impressions and no clicks is
probably navigational — someone searched the brand, saw it, and did not
need to click. Worth checking, not worth panicking about.

queries and they are formatted as a question a person would type.

## The daily timeline

Merged from all three exports, 2026-06-26 → 2026-08-15:

- 1,315 impressions, 14 clicks, **1.06% CTR**
- Impressions climbed from 0-4 a day at the end of June to 30-70 a day
  through August
- Clicks stayed at 0-2 a day throughout
- The jump on 2026-07-10 (42 impressions in a day) is the senior-tech
  roundups being published

Impressions are growing. Clicks are not, because the ranking is not.

## What to do, in order

1. **Link the cloud cluster together.** The new "Cloud for Cheap" post
   and the old year-end post need to reference each other, and the old
   one needs a line pointing forward to the new one. Right now they are
   two posts about the same thing with nothing between them.
2. **Write the Bitwarden/Vaultwarden extension post.** Three of the
   top-10 queries are about the browser extension, including one
   troubleshooting query with no post behind it. Ranked already.
3. **Write the iPhone accessibility post.** "senior mode iphone",
   "how to make iphone more accessible for elderly" and "best
   smartphone for seniors 2026" are all top-13. The iPhone setup post
   has the material; it does not have the search-matching title.
4. **Add one more "Best X for Y in 2026" roundup.** That format is
   three for three on clicks.
5. **Stop writing posts for year-end deadlines.** Every query with
   "2025" in it is a query from a stale page.

whether the dev.to cross-posts send anyone.
